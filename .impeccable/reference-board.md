## 2. Design Decisions Derived for BDN WORKATION 001

1. **Palette & Surface Hierarchy (Pinned):**
   - Primary ground: **Warm paper (`#F3F0E8`)** across the main reading flow for Kinfolk/editorial calm.
   - Primary ink: **Charcoal (`#20231F`)** (contrast ratio >13:1 on `#F3F0E8`).
   - Structural dividers & quiet surfaces: **Sand (`#C6BBA8`)** for `1px` editorial hairlines, provisional detail tables, and subtle framed blocks.
   - Restrained accents: **Volcanic green (`#75806B`)** for secondary chapter/editorial bands & subtle nature metadata; **Solar clay (`#D68A50`)** reserved intentionally for primary CTA emphasis and key editorial warmth (always paired with `#20231F` dark ink or deep contrast text so WCAG AA >=4.5:1 holds).
2. **Typography Hierarchy (Pinned):**
   - **Display (`Instrument Serif`):** Editorial headlines (`Work somewhere else. Connect differently.`, chapter titles, final statement `"Good people. New places. Different perspectives."`), set with tight editorial leading (`0.98–1.05`) and balanced wrapping (`text-wrap: balance`).
   - **Body (`Manrope`):** Clean, legible warm humanist sans at `1rem–1.125rem` (`1.65` line-height, `58–68ch` measure).
   - **Metadata (`IBM Plex Mono`):** Dates (`24–30 JAN 2027`), duration (`7 DAYS`), capacity (`~40 NOMADS — PROVISIONAL`), chapter indices (`01`–`06`), and explicit provisional disclaimers.
3. **Layout & Anti-Slop Discipline:**
   - **12-column editorial grid** (`max-width: 1360px`, `24px` gutter on desktop, collapsing cleanly to single-column with `16px–20px` side padding on `390px` mobile).
   - **Section 03 (The Experience):** No 3-column equal cards! Instead, three full-width **editorial chapters** separated by `1px` `#C6BBA8` hairlines with alternating asymmetric proportions (`Work together.`, `Explore together.`, `Make memories together.`) pairing prose with clearly labeled Gran Canaria destination photography / marked villa placeholders.
   - **Section 05 (The Details):** Hairline-ruled editorial ledger (inspired by *Builders Table* & print prospectuses) showing proposed dates, duration, provisional accommodation tiers (`€599` Shared / `€749` Private), and an unmistakable provisional/non-booking disclaimer.
   - **Navigation & Mobile CTA:** Lightweight sticky top bar (`56px–64px`) with `BDN · 001` mark, 4 concise anchor links (`Idea`, `Experience`, `Destination`, `Details`), and `Express Interest` CTA; plus a clean mobile bottom/header CTA path without clutter.
4. **Content & Asset Integrity:**
   - Use **verified Gran Canaria photography** (the official BDN Agaete photo + verified CC-licensed Wikimedia Commons photos of Roque Nublo, Tejeda Caldera sunset, Tamadaba sea cliffs, Las Canteras, and Vegueta basalt architecture) strictly captioned by their real location.
   - **Never invent villa interiors or unconfirmed property features:** any villa-specific media slot uses an explicit editorial architectural frame labeled `PROVISIONAL VILLA PLACEHOLDER · FINAL PROPERTY & PHOTOS TBC`.
