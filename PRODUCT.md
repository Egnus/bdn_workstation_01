# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

SvelteKit + Svelte 5 + Tailwind CSS v4 (lucide-svelte icons, shadcn-svelte only when genuinely useful; package manager: pnpm / pnpx only — never npm or npx; target: Vercel static/SSG deployment; no database, auth, or custom backend)

## Users

Barcelona Digital Nomads (BDN) community members—remote workers, founders, creatives, and digital nomads—considering a 7-day shared workation in Gran Canaria to work remotely alongside familiar BDN faces, meet new people, and explore the island together.

## Product Purpose

An editorial-style, single-page campaign introducing the first-ever BDN Workation (`BDN WORKATION 001` in Gran Canaria, 24–30 January 2027, 7 days, ~40 participants). It communicates the concept, community atmosphere, editorial experience chapters, destination character, and provisional trip details while driving visitors to submit the existing interest survey (`https://bdnforms.lovable.app/workation-form`).

## Positioning

A community-rooted workation organized by Barcelona Digital Nomads—not a commercial coliving package or open booking platform—combining focused remote work with island exploration and shared evenings alongside an existing Barcelona community.

## Operating Context

- Expression-of-interest campaign only; bookings and deposits are NOT open yet.
- Primary conversion: `https://bdnforms.lovable.app/workation-form` (`EXPRESS YOUR INTEREST` in Hero, `JOIN THE INTEREST LIST` in Final CTA, plus accessible header/mobile CTA).
- Six-section information architecture:
  - Section 01 — Hero: `"Work somewhere else. Connect differently."` + BDN Workation 001, Gran Canaria, 24–30 January 2027, Seven days, ~40 people, CTA `EXPRESS YOUR INTEREST`.
  - Section 02 — The Idea: personal, welcoming, confident intro explaining why BDN is organizing this trip.
  - Section 03 — The Experience: three editorial chapters (`Work together.`, `Explore together.`, `Make memories together.`) avoiding repetitive feature cards.
  - Section 04 — The Destination: Gran Canaria through architecture, nature, landscape, and local atmosphere.
  - Section 05 — The Details: provisional dates (24–30 January 2027), duration (7 days), room options & indicative prices (`Shared room: €599`, `Private room: €749`), clearly marked provisional/subject to confirmation and not accepting bookings/deposits.
  - Section 06 — Final CTA: `"Good people. New places. Different perspectives."` + CTA `JOIN THE INTEREST LIST` linking to `https://bdnforms.lovable.app/workation-form`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: English.
- Content & Asset Integrity: Never invent villa photographs, confirmed accommodation features, exact property location, unconfirmed activities, booking availability, or final prices/conditions. Use clearly identified placeholders where property details are unavailable, and note that 40 participants is the public working capacity subject to organizer confirmation.
- No scroll hijacking, excessive parallax, infinite decorative animations, intrusive custom cursors, purple/blue AI gradients, generic icon-card grids, or decorative pills/badges.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual identity (pinned): Contemporary Editorial / Boutique Travel (independent travel magazine aesthetics, Kinfolk-inspired editorial typography, boutique hospitality photography, minimalist art direction).
- Pinned Palette: Warm paper `#F3F0E8` (main background), Charcoal `#20231F` (main ink), Volcanic green `#75806B`, Solar clay `#D68A50`, Sand `#C6BBA8`.
- Pinned Typography: Display `Instrument Serif`, Body `Manrope`, Metadata `IBM Plex Mono`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Official BDN community post (Gran Canaria, 1-week villa workation, 40 digital nomads, coworking + evening memories).
- Official interest survey at `https://bdnforms.lovable.app/workation-form` (Agaete Gran Canaria photo, 24–30 January 2027, €599 shared twin / €749 private indicative tiers, future €100 refundable deposit concept, exploratory activity options).

## Product Principles

- Every visual decision serves editorial clarity and genuine hospitality storytelling—fewer, better-crafted elements over generic components.
- Respect content integrity strictly: clearly distinguish provisional survey details from confirmed facts and clearly label placeholder media where villa specifics do not exist yet.
- Pair calm editorial typography (`Instrument Serif` + `Manrope` + `IBM Plex Mono`) with crisp, accessible interactions and responsive mobile-first craft.
